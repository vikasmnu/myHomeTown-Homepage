Visit Gurugram

Gurugram perfectly bridges its sacred, ancient past with a hyper-futuristic reality.

Top three activities to do at Gurugram:

Visit Sheetla Mata Temple

Shri Mata Sheetla Devi Mandir is one of the most revered and historic Hindu pilgrimage sites in Haryana.

Visit Ambience Mall

Ambience Mall in Gurugram is one of India's largest and most vibrant premium shopping destinations.

Visit Aravali hill range

The Aravalli Range (also spelled Aravali) is one of the oldest geological fold mountain ranges in the world.
